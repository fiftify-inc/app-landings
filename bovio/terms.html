1. What Bovio is

Bovio is a Discord application that brings the "Bulls and Cows" code-guessing game to Discord servers. The free tier offers the core 4-digit game, personal stats, server leaderboards, and a global daily challenge. The premium tier, sold per-server through Discord's monetization system, unlocks additional difficulties, gameplay modes, tournaments, and customization.
