# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-11_

StatStation is built to know as little about your server as possible.

**Short version:** the bot requests the Discord **Guilds** intent only. It cannot read your messages or your member list. We store a handful of configuration IDs and your subscription state.

## 1. What we store
- Discord guild ID
- Configured union IDs
- Channel IDs
- Post times and server timezone
- Subscription / entitlement state (as reported by Discord)

## 2. What we do not collect
- No message content
- No member lists, roles, or profiles
- No personal data about Discord users
- No DMs, voice, or attachments

Because the bot holds the Guilds intent only, this data is not accessible to the Service in the first place.

## 3. Game statistics
TS2 figures are aggregated public leaderboard data collected by Fiftify. They are not tied to your Discord identity.

## 4. How we use what we store
Solely to operate the Service. We do not sell data, build advertising profiles, or use it for any other purpose.

## 5. Third-party services
- Discord API - install, post reports, manage subscriptions
- Cloudflare - website hosting
- PostgreSQL on our own infrastructure - stores the configuration above

No advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.

## 6. Retention
Configuration is kept while StatStation is installed and active, and deleted within a reasonable period after removal. Request earlier deletion via support.

## 7. Your choices
Configure or remove the bot to control the data. For access or deletion requests: info@fiftify.com.

## 8. Children
Not directed to children under 13 (or the local minimum age); no knowing collection of their data.

## 9. Changes
Updates are reflected by the "Last updated" date.
