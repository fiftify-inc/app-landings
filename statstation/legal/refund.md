# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-11_

**Read this first.** StatStation subscriptions are sold through Discord Premium Apps, which makes **Discord the merchant of record**. Payments, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Discord under Discord's own policies - not billed or refunded by us directly.

## 1. Cancelling a subscription
- Open **User Settings → Subscriptions** in Discord.
- Cancel the StatStation subscription.
- Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period; it will not renew.

Cancelling stops future charges. It does not, by itself, issue a refund for the current period.

## 2. Refunds
Refund eligibility and any refund are determined by **Discord's refund policy and process**. Request a refund through Discord's support / billing flow. We cannot charge or refund your payment method directly.

## 3. Free demo
The top-50 demo digest is free - nothing to refund.

## 4. If something went wrong
If StatStation failed to deliver what your plan promised, contact us. We will investigate, help fix the configuration, and where appropriate support your refund request to Discord.
