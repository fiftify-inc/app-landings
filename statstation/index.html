Trust & privacy

It reads leaderboards, not your server.

Guilds intent only

The bot requests one intent. It cannot read your messages, your member list, or anything else in your server. Ever.

Almost nothing stored

Guild ID, the union IDs you track, channel IDs, post times, timezone and subscription state. No message content, no personal data.

What it doesn't do

Data covers the top ~25 players per union and the top-100 unions - not full rosters. Forecasts are pace projections, an estimate, not a promise.

StatStation is a third-party analytics tool for TS2 players. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's publisher, and shows only aggregated public leaderboard data.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Which unions are supported?
Any union inside the TS2 top-100. The underlying data covers roughly the top 25 players of each tracked union.
How fresh is the data?
Every figure is for the last completed day of the season and refreshes once per day. Your report posts on the schedule you set.
What if my union isn't tracked?
If your union is outside the top-100 it can't be tracked yet. You can still run the free top-50 demo digest in the meantime.
How do I cancel?
Billing runs through Discord's Premium Apps. Cancel any time from Discord's subscription settings - you keep access until the current period ends.
Do you store my data?
Only your Discord guild ID, the union IDs you track, channel IDs, post times, timezone and subscription state. No message content, no member lists, no personal data. The bot requests the Guilds intent only.
